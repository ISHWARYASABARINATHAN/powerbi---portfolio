## Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition patterns for 1470 employees to identify key factors driving turnover. It helps HR teams understand which departments, roles, and demographics are most at risk.

- **Presented By:** Ishwarya S

## Key KPIs
- **239** - Employees Left
- **701** - Male Employees  
- **273** - Single Employees
- **16%** - Attrition Rate

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Cover Page:** Title & Project Info
2. **Attrition Overview:** Department, Job Role, Age Group wise attrition analysis
3. **Deep Dive:** Gender-wise, Overtime vs Attrition, Income vs Attrition analysis
4. **Key Findings:** Insights & Data Story

## Key Insights
- Attrition is concentrated among younger employees (26-35 age group) in frontline roles, particularly in R&D and Sales. 
- Males (63.29%) are more likely to leave than females (36.71%).
- Overtime plus low promotion opportunities are strongly linked to turnover.
- Employees who left also earned less on average, suggesting workload balance, career growth, and competitive pay are critical areas for HR to address.
