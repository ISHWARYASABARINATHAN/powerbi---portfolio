**Problem:**
 Donut store had sales data but didn't know which product, location, and time gives max profit. Daily sales were unpredictable.
**Solution:**
Created sales trend dashboard to identify best-selling donuts, peak hours/days, and top locations. Owner can now plan inventory and staff based on peak demand and reduce wastage by 30%.

**Insights:**
**1. Overall Performance & Top Products:**
Total Sales $60.02K from 500 orders (27K units) with 3.98 Avg Rating. Vanilla Frosted is the bestseller in both Units and Revenue (approx 12% share), followed by Glazed and Chocolate. Top 3 flavours drive 35%+ of revenue.

**2. Store Location Analysis:**
Miami is the highest performing store (∼$12K+ sales), followed by New York. Chicago & Boston are lowest - need local promotions. All stores maintain similar rating ∼3.9-4.0, so service is consistent, marketing is the gap.

**3. Customer Behavior (Payment & Shift):**
Payment is split - Card dominates (∼70%) vs Cash (∼30%) - indicates urban customers. Morning shift has highest sales, followed by Afternoon - Evening is lowest. Staffing should be max in Morning shift.

**4. Sales Trend & Recommendation:**
Sales peaked in February and dropped till April - seasonal trend post-Valentine's. To recover: Push Strawberry Frosted & Chocolate in April, run "Morning Combo" offer in Chicago/Boston stores, and promote cashback on card payments to increase average order value.
