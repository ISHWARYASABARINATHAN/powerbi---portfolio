**03.Student Lifestyle Analysis**

**Screenshots:**
[Student Lifestyle Analysis] [https://github.com/ISHWARYASABARINATHAN/powerbi---portfolio/blob/main/03.student%20lifestyle%20analysis/student%20lifestyle%20dashboard.png]

**Problem:** 
College admin wanted to understand how lifestyle (sleep, study hours, diet, social media) affects student CGPA and mental health, but data was scattered.

**Solution:**
Built lifestyle vs performance dashboard showing correlation between study hours, sleep, and CGPA. Helps counselors identify at-risk students and suggest better routines for improved grades

**Insights:**
* Students with <6 hours sleep have 40% lower GPA.
* High Stress group avg GPA = 6.2 vs Low Stress = 8.1.
*  7-8 hours study = Highest pass rate  (2000 students).
*  Increase sleep to 7.5 to boost performance.
