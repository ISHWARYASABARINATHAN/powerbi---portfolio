# 🎓 Student Lifestyle Analysis Dashboard | 2000 Students

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the impact of Sleep, Study Hours, and Stress Level on Student GPA and Academic Performance using Agentic AI.

### 2️⃣ Dashboard Overview - KPIs & Insights
![Dashboard](https://github.com/ISHWARYASABARINATHAN/powerbi---portfolio/blob/main/3.Student-Lifestyle-Analysis/2.Dashboard.png)

**KPIs:**
- **Average Sleep Hours:** 7.50
- **Pass Students Count:** 2000
- **Average Study Hours:** 7.48

**Visuals Analyzed:**
- GPA by Sleep Hours (Column Chart)
- Result by Stress_Level (Pie Chart - High, Moderate, Low)
- GPA by Stress Level (Bar Chart)
- Slicers: Stress_Level, Performance Level

**Insights on Dashboard:**
- Key Finding: Students with <6 hours sleep have 40% lower GPA.
- Stress Impact: High Stress group avg GPA = 6.2 vs Low Stress = 8.1
- Study Sweet Spot: 7-8 hours study = Highest pass rate (2000 students).
- Recommendation: Increase sleep to 7.5 to boost performance.

## 💡 Key Insights Summary

1. **Sleep is Critical:** 7.5 hours is the ideal average. Below 6 hours drops GPA by 40%.
2. **Stress Kills Performance:** Low Stress students score 8.1 GPA vs High Stress 6.2 GPA.
3. **Study Balance:** 7-8 hours study is the sweet spot for maximum pass rate.
