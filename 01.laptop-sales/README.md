**Problem:** Laptop shop owner didn't know which brand, price, and specs customers buy most, leading to overstock of low-demand laptops.
**Solution:** Analyzed 1000+ laptops to find best-selling price range (₹70-75K), top brands (Dell/Lenovo/HP 60%), and most demanded specs (8GB RAM + 256GB SSD). Helps owner stock only high-demand models.

**Inssights:**
**1. Pricing Insight :**

Average laptop price is ₹59.9K, with most laptops ranging from ₹35K-₹80K. Gaming laptops priced 2x higher than Ultra books.

**2. Brand & Type :**

Dell, Lenovo, HP dominate with 60% market share. Notebook category is most common (55%), followed by Gaming (20%).

**3. Specifications :**

8GB RAM is standard (45% laptops). 256GB SSD is most popular storage. Intel Core i5 is the most used CPU.

**4. Recommendation :**

 Focus on mid-range (₹50K-₹70K) i5 + 8GB RAM + 256GB SSD combo - best value for students & office users.
