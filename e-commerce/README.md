# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyse revenue trends, sales performance, customer transactions, and country-wise business contributions.

The dashboard provides business stakeholders with a clear view of sales metrics, helping identify high-performing markets, revenue trends, and opportunities for business growth through data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyse revenue trends across different countries.
- Track sales quantity and invoice activity.
- Identify top contributing markets.
- Evaluate year-over-year revenue growth.
- Support strategic business expansion decisions.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

- Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Interactive Dashboards

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks essential sales metrics including:

- Average Revenue: **40.16**
- Total Revenue: **84.33K**
- Total Quantity Sold: **40K**
- Total Invoices: **2,100**

---

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically explore data using:

- Country Slicer
- Invoice Date Range Filter

---

### Visualisations

#### Revenue by Country
- Displays the contribution of each country to total revenue.
- Helps identify dominant and underperforming markets.

#### Country Revenue Distribution
- Donut chart showing revenue share by country.
- Highlights major revenue-generating regions.

#### Revenue Trend by Year
- Line chart analysing revenue growth over time.
- Useful for monitoring business performance and forecasting.

#### Sales Matrix
- Detailed breakdown of transactions by country and invoice number.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

✅ The United Kingdom contributes approximately **89%** of total revenue, making it the primary revenue-generating market.

✅ Revenue increased significantly from **2010 to 2011**, indicating strong business growth.

✅ Over **40,000 units** were sold through **2,100 invoices**, demonstrating a healthy transaction volume.

✅ Revenue concentration in a single market creates dependency risk for the business.

✅ Several countries contribute only a small percentage of revenue, indicating opportunities for market expansion.

✅ Average revenue per unit remains relatively modest, suggesting further analysis of pricing strategy and product mix.

---

## 📊 Business Recommendations

- Diversify revenue sources by expanding into underperforming countries.
- Reduce dependency on the UK market.
- Review pricing strategies to improve average revenue per unit.
- Identify successful products and replicate performance across other regions.
- Invest in marketing and customer acquisition efforts in emerging markets.
- Monitor country-level performance regularly to support strategic decision-making.
