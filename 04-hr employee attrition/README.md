# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **HR Employee Attrition Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to analyze employee turnover patterns and identify the key factors influencing attrition.

The dashboard helps HR teams and decision-makers understand workforce trends, monitor employee satisfaction, and develop retention strategies based on data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across departments, job roles, and demographics.
- Identify factors contributing to employee turnover.
- Track important HR KPIs.
- Provide actionable insights to improve employee retention.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

- **Power BI**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation**
- **Interactive Visualizations**

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

- Total Records: **1470 Employees**

  - Employee Demographics

### 3️⃣ Key Insights

The dashboard revealed several important findings:

✅ Attrition is concentrated among younger employees.

✅ Research & Development and Sales departments experience higher employee turnover.

✅ Frontline roles such as Laboratory Technicians show higher attrition rates.

✅ Employees working overtime are more likely to leave the organization.

✅ Male employees have slightly higher attrition compared to female employees.

✅ Employees with fewer promotion opportunities tend to leave more frequently.

✅ Employees who left the company generally earned lower average incomes.

---

## 📊 Key Business Recommendations

- Improve career growth and promotion opportunities.
- Monitor employee workload and overtime.
- Strengthen retention programs for younger employees.
- Review compensation structures for high-risk roles.
- Increase engagement initiatives within high-attrition departments.
- Enhance work-life balance programs.
